Five phases of delivery

Structured the PRINCE2® way.

Every project is run to PRINCE2, the internationally recognised project management method, adapted for construction. Each phase has a clear purpose, defined outputs and a decision point, so you always know where your project stands and nothing proceeds without your approval.

01

Start up

  • Project brief & requirements
  • Feasibility & budget benchmarking
  • Outline business case
  • Delivery approach & team
02

Initiate & plan

  • Project initiation document
  • Budget, programme & risk register
  • Design team & approvals
  • Procurement, tender & contract award
03

Deliver & control

  • Owner's rep on site
  • Contractor work packages
  • RFIs, variations & issues
  • Monthly cost & highlight reports
  • Programme, quality & WHS
04

Commission & hand over

  • Pre-PC inspections & defects
  • Commissioning & testing
  • As-built & O&M review
  • Practical completion
  • Keys, warranties & handover
05

Close & review

  • Formal client acceptance
  • Defects liability oversight
  • Final account negotiation
  • End project report
  • Lessons learned
Throughout every phase

You direct. We manage.

You make the key decisions, as the PRINCE2 project board would, and we agree tolerances for cost and time up front. Anything forecast to exceed them is escalated to you straight away.

Stage gates.

At the end of each phase you get an end-stage report with an updated budget, programme, risks and business case, plus a clear recommendation. The next phase starts only with your go-ahead.
